Pass The Popcorn privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026
Iteration18 Software Solutions Inc. (“Iteration 18”, “we”, “us”)

Pass The Popcorn is a media player. It plays live television and on-demand video from a server address you enter. We do not supply, host, or license any video. You choose the server. You are responsible for having the right to use it and for what you play. This policy explains what information the app handles and how we treat it.

AI

We use AI tools to develop Pass The Popcorn. The app does not have AI features inside it today. Nothing you watch or type is sent to a language-model provider. We do not use AI for advertising.

Who this covers

Pass The Popcorn is a consumer app. The license belongs to the Pass The Popcorn account you sign in with. Anyone who signs in with that account on a device uses one of the seats on the license. The server you connect is not operated by us.

Content

Pass The Popcorn is only a player. It does not include a video catalog of its own. Any channels or titles that appear come from the server you configured. We do not review that material and we are not responsible for it, for the server you choose, or for how you use the app. You must only connect a server you are authorized to use and only play material you have the right to play.

Information the app uses

Depending on how you use Pass The Popcorn, the app may process:

  • Account details. Email address and password for sign-in.
  • License and devices. Whether the account has an active license, the product (lifetime or monthly), how many seat packs it has, a device identifier, a short device label (such as the phone model), when that device last signed in, and the country of the connection used to check the license.
  • Purchases. That a purchase completed, which product it was, and a store identifier for that purchase. We do not receive or store card numbers. Payment is handled by Google Play on Android and by Stripe on Windows.
  • Server you connect. The address, username, and password you enter so the player can open that server. The password is kept in the device’s secure store. Those details stay on the device and are sent only to the server you named. We do not host video.
  • Playback on this account. What you play and items you mark, kept on the device and synced in encrypted form so your other signed-in devices can pick it up. We cannot read that activity in plain text on our servers.
  • Notifications. A push-notification token on Android, used only if notifications are enabled.

The Android app requests network access so it can sign in, check the license, and reach the server you entered. Additional device permissions are requested only if a feature needs them.

How we use it

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in on this device.
  • Confirm a purchase and keep the license current, including renewals, cancellations, and refunds from the store that billed it.
  • Give each device a seat and stop playback when a seat or stream lease is no longer valid.
  • Use the country of the connection to enforce the license.
  • Open media from the server you entered.
  • Sync encrypted playback activity across your signed-in devices.
  • Send a notice to your account email when the license needs attention.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the app for advertising.

Where data is stored

Sign-in, the license, device seats, and encrypted activity are stored with our hosting providers, currently Supabase. A local copy of settings, credentials, and activity stays on the device. Country for a license check may be read from the request or looked up from the connection.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the product:

  • Infrastructure. Hosting and authentication (currently Supabase), and Firebase for Android push notifications.
  • Payments. Google Play or Stripe, depending on which store you buy through. They process the payment; we receive confirmation so we can write the license.
  • The server you entered. The address and credentials you typed are sent from the device to that server so it can return a catalog and media.
  • Email. Account notices are sent through our email provider (currently Resend) to the address on the account.
  • Legal requests. If we are required by law to disclose information.

Other people signed in with the same Pass The Popcorn account can use the remaining seats and see the same license. We do not show one account’s data to another account.

Retention

We keep the account, license, and device list while the account is active, and as needed for backups, security, and legal obligations. You can remove a device from the license in the app. To request deletion of an account and its license, email steve@iteration18.com.

Security

Access requires an account. Data in transit uses encrypted connections. Watch activity is encrypted on the device before it is synced. Server passwords stay in the device secure store. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Children

Pass The Popcorn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can sign out, remove a device, or stop using the app. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, email steve@iteration18.com. If you are in Canada, you may also have rights under applicable privacy law, including PIPEDA.
