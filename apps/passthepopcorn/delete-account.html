Delete your Pass The Popcorn account

Pass The Popcorn by Iteration18 Software Solutions Inc.
Effective November 12, 2026

This page is for people who signed in to Pass The Popcorn (the Android and Windows media player published by Iteration 18). If you ask us to delete your account, we permanently delete all account data we hold for that account. There is no extra retention period after the request is completed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email steve@iteration18.com from the same address you use to sign in to Pass The Popcorn.
  2. Use the subject line Delete Pass The Popcorn account and include that email address in the message.
  3. We confirm the request belongs to that account, then permanently delete the account and all associated data we hold.
  4. Uninstall Pass The Popcorn from each device so any local copy of settings and credentials on that device is removed as well.

You can also write to us by post: Iteration18 Software Solutions Inc., 208 King St E, Saint John, NB E2L 1H3, Canada.

What we delete

When the request is completed, the following is permanently deleted:

  • Your sign-in account (email and authentication records).
  • License and seats: product, status, packs, device identifiers, device labels, last-seen times, and related license checks.
  • Encrypted playback activity synced for that account (watch history and similar marks).
  • Push-notification tokens stored for that account.
  • Server connection details we hold in the account, if any. The password for your media server is kept on the device, not on our servers; uninstalling the app removes that local copy.

What we do not delete

  • Nothing of yours is kept on our side after deletion. We do not retain a copy of the account for later use.
  • Purchase records at Google Play or Stripe stay with those companies as their payment systems require. We do not store card numbers. Cancel a subscription in Play or Stripe if you still have one before or after you write to us.
  • The media server you connected is not operated by us. Catalog and video there are not our account data and are not deleted by this request.

Timing

We process requests as soon as we can confirm they come from the account holder. Deletion is permanent. After it is done, you cannot sign in with that account again unless you create a new one.
